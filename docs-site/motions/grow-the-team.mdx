<Note>
  **Agents distribute behaviors; they do not manage people.** `invite` is the one roster verb in the
  CLI. Reading the roster, changing roles, removing members, and leaving a workspace are browser
  acts, deliberately.
</Note>

## Invite

Inviting is **owner-only** and **requires mail to be configured** — the mailbox round-trip *is* the
identity proof.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="CLI">
    ```bash
    topos invite dev@acme.com ops@acme.com             # describe
    topos invite dev@acme.com --channel backend --yes  # apply, with a first destination
    topos invite deploy@acme.com --skill deploy --yes
    topos invite                                       # read-only: the address and the policy
    ```

    At most one of `--skill` / `--channel`. The hint prefills the newcomer's profile when they accept,
    so they land already carrying the set.

    A bare `invite` with no addresses mutates nothing and says so — it prints the workspace address
    and the invite policy.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Browser">
    On the **Members** page: **Invite by email** takes several addresses separated by spaces or
    commas.

    > Invitations lapse after 7 days. Inviting an address again re-sends the mail and re-arms the
    > clock.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

**Every invitee starts as a member.** There is no role picker on the invite form — roles are raised
afterwards on the roster.

The invitation token travels only in the mail. The inviter never sees the link.

## What the invitee does

The mail carries three routes, in order: a browser link, a paste-block for their agent, and
`topos login <invite-url>`.

Opening the link shows who invited them, which workspace, the first skill or channel if one was
hinted, and that joining seats them and delivers the team's skills to their agents.

The accept is **email-bound**:

| Their situation | What they see |
|---|---|
| Signed in as the invited address | One **Accept invitation** click |
| Signed in as someone else | Both addresses named, and **Sign out and continue as \<email\>** — it never accepts as the wrong account |
| Not signed in, account exists | **Sign in as \<email\> to accept** |
| Not signed in, no account | An account mint, born verified — the token arrived in that mailbox, which is the proof |
| Mailbox not yet verified | A prompt to send the verification mail first |

They can also **decline**, which is recorded and visible to the inviter, and re-invitable.

Every dead token — invalid, expired, revoked, already used — renders one constant page naming neither
the workspace nor the email.

Accepting applies the hint as a profile line. **Nothing lands on any machine from a browser accept** —
bytes arrive when they run `topos login` on a machine.

## Roles

On the **Members** page, owner-only, behind a **Change role** control with a two-step confirm.

| Role | Adds |
|---|---|
| **member** | Self-scoped verbs; publish to open skills; create channels; curate open channels |
| **reviewer** | Approve/reject proposals, roll back, curate curated channels, see everyone's sessions |
| **owner** | Invitations, roles, removal, loosening protection, archive/delete, workspace settings |

**Ownership transfers by promotion:** make someone else an owner first. The workspace must always
keep an owner, so the last owner can be neither demoted nor removed nor allowed to leave.

## Pending invitations

Visible to every member on the Members page; revoking is owner-only.

Each row shows the address, a `pending` or `declined` chip, who invited them, and when it lapses
(`lapses in 6 hours`, `lapses in 3 days`, `lapsed`).

There is no separate resend control — **re-inviting is resending**, and it mints a fresh link that
kills the old one.

## Remove access

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Remove a member — owner, Members page">
    Removing the seat also **ends that person's sessions in this workspace**, in the same
    transaction. Two-step confirm; the last owner cannot be removed.

    Their local copies stay theirs. Removal stops future delivery; it does not reach onto a machine
    and delete files.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Remove one session — owner, Settings → Sessions">
    Ends delivery and reporting for exactly that machine-and-workspace pair.

    > The copies already on the machine stay put and must be chased by hand where it matters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Leave — anyone, on their own seat">
    > Leaving removes your seat and cuts off every one of your machines from this workspace. The
    > local copies you already hold stay yours.

    A sole owner is refused until they promote someone else.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Watch the fleet

**Settings → Sessions** is where a lead answers *"is everyone current? did the revert land?"*

Owners and reviewers see the whole workspace grouped by person; a member sees only their own.

Each session shows its machine name, a status chip with an explaining tooltip, when it logged in and
was last seen, and its **per-skill applied state** — `current` or `behind`, with the applied version
id.

| Chip | Means |
|---|---|
| **fresh** | Reported within the staleness window |
| **stale** | No report within the window. Sessions report when an agent runs, so an idle machine reads stale — unconfirmed, not wrong |
| **never reported** | Logged in, but nothing has synced on it yet |
| **expired** | Past the workspace's session expiry — the machine logs in again |

Owners can **Approve**, **Reject**, and **Remove** sessions, each with a two-step confirm. People end
their own from `/account/sessions` or with `topos logout`.

## Workspace policy

Owners, on **Settings → General**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Review gate** | off | Require review for every change — the default unpinned skills inherit |
| **Staleness window** | 7 days | When a session reads *stale* on the sessions page (1 hour – 366 days) |
| **Session approval** | off | New member logins wait for an owner's approval. An owner's own session is always born active |
| **Session expiry** | none | How long a login lasts before the machine logs in again (1 hour – 366 days) |
| **Registration** | invite-only | *Self-hosted single-workspace installs only.* Whether sign-up needs an invitation |
| **Export skills** | — | Downloads every skill at its current version plus a manifest, as one zip |

Each knob shows its own history line — *"Last set: 14 days, by Priya, 3 weeks ago"* — read from the
audit ledger. Refused attempts are recorded but never pollute it.
